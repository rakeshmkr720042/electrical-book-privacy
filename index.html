Privacy Policy: Electrical Engineering Hub

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Electrical Engineering Hub ("the app") is an educational app by Mr. Rakesh (Smart Mistri). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences (language, theme) and your study progress locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet, ads and analytics

The app's study content is bundled inside the app. It does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not send data to third parties.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and above and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
